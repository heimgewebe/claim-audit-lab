First decide whether the claim is about what the source reports or about what happened in the world. CONTRADICTED requires an explicit or logically entailed opposite claim, or an incompatible value for the SAME referent. Not measuring, examining, mentioning, confirming, or proving a claim is INSUFFICIENT rather than CONTRADICTED for a world claim. Saying a study drew no causal conclusion contradicts a claim that the STUDY drew one, but does not disprove that the causal relation exists. Do not promote mere absence of evidence to evidence of absence.
